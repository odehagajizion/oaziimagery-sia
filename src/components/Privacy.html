PRIVACY NOTICE

Your details, handled with care.

This notice explains what personal information OAZI IMAGERY collects through this website, why, and the choices you have. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Last updated 26 November 2026

Who we are

OAZI IMAGERY (RC-7806774) is a strategic visual communications company based in Abuja, Nigeria, and part of OAZI GROUP. We are responsible for the personal information described here. For anything about your data, email info@oaziimagery.org or call +234 706 924 2191.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through our contact form, we collect:

  • your name, organisation and email address;
  • your phone or WhatsApp number, if you give it;
  • your sector, the services you are interested in, and your message;
  • the discovery call day and time you choose;
  • whether you asked for WhatsApp messages.

Our hosting provider also keeps standard technical logs, such as your IP address and browser type, to run the website securely. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and this website sets no cookies.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you and arrange your call. We use your details because you asked us to contact you, which is a step you take before any agreement with us.
  • To send WhatsApp messages. We send your call confirmation and reminder on WhatsApp only if you tick that box. Email is always sent as well.
  • To keep the website safe. Technical logs help us stop spam and abuse, which is in our legitimate interest.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the service providers that help us run this website and reply to you:

  • Netlify hosts this website and stores contact form enquiries.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on these pages, so your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Email and WhatsApp providers carry the messages we send you.

Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria. When that happens, we rely on the safeguards the Nigeria Data Protection Act requires, such as the provider's contractual data protection commitments.

Links to YouTube, LinkedIn and Instagram take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

If your enquiry does not lead to a project, we delete it within 12 months. If you become a client, your details become part of your project records and are kept as our client agreement and the law require.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information, or stop using it;
  • send your information to you or another organisation in a usable format.

You can also withdraw your consent at any time, for example to WhatsApp messages. Email info@oaziimagery.org and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at ndpc.gov.ng.

Keeping it secure

This website is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and access to enquiries is limited to the people at OAZI IMAGERY who need it to reply to you.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
